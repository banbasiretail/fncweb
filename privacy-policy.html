01Information We Collect

At the current time, Free News Club does not collect, store, or require users to provide personal information. The App currently does not require:

  • User registration or account creation
  • Login credentials
  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address
  • Precise location information
  • Contacts
  • Photos or media from your device
  • Personal files
  • Payment information
  • User-generated content
  • Passwords or authentication information

The App also does not currently provide features such as user profiles, favorites, bookmarks, or personalized user accounts.

02Device Permissions

Free News Club currently requests only one Android permission:

INTERNET

This permission is required so the App can connect to online services and retrieve/display content provided through the YouTube API and related online resources.

Free News Club does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, storage, phone information, or other sensitive device resources.

03YouTube API Services

Free News Club uses YouTube API Services to retrieve publicly available information about selected YouTube channels and their videos, and presents that content in an organized interface to make it easier to discover and view news and educational videos from selected channels.

The video and channel content displayed through the App is provided by YouTube and/or the respective content creators. Free News Club does not claim ownership of third-party YouTube content. When you watch YouTube videos through the App, that content remains subject to YouTube's applicable terms, policies, and the rights of the respective content owners.

YouTube API Services are provided by YouTube, and their use is subject to the applicable YouTube API Services Terms of Service and Developer Policies. You can review:

  • YouTube Terms of Service
  • YouTube API Services Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy

Free News Club is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by YouTube unless explicitly stated otherwise.

04Third-Party Content

Free News Club provides access to and displays content originating from third-party YouTube channels. We do not own or claim ownership of the videos, channel names, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, or other third-party content made available through YouTube. All applicable rights in third-party content remain with their respective owners or licensors.

If you believe that any content displayed through the App infringes your rights, you may contact us using the information in the Contact Us section below. We will review legitimate concerns and take appropriate action where applicable.

05Data Storage

Free News Club currently does not maintain user accounts or store personal user information on our servers. The current version of the App also does not intentionally store personal information locally on your device.

The App may temporarily process information necessary to retrieve and display online content, including information received from the YouTube API, as required for the normal operation of the App.

07Analytics and Crash Reporting

Firebase AnalyticsFirebase Crashlytics

08Notifications

The current version of Free News Club does not send push notifications and does not request notification-related permissions. If notification functionality is introduced in a future version, the applicable privacy practices will be described in an updated version of this Privacy Policy.

09Children's Privacy

Free News Club is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. Because the current version of the App does not require registration or collect personal information, users can access the App without providing personal information to us.

10Third-Party Services

The App may interact with third-party services, particularly YouTube API Services, in order to retrieve and display content. Third-party services may have their own terms of service and privacy policies. We encourage users to review the privacy policies and terms of any third-party service they interact with.

For information about how Google handles information in connection with its services, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

11Data Security

Although Free News Club currently does not collect personal user information, we take reasonable measures to maintain the security and integrity of the App and its connections to online services. No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
